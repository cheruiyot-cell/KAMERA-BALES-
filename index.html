Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know the bale quality is consistent?

Every bale is hand‑graded by our team with 15+ years of experience. We photograph and document each bale, and if you receive anything below the stated grade, we offer a replacement or refund.

What if I receive damaged or unsellable items?

Our return policy covers up to 10% of defective items. Simply send photos within 48 hours of delivery, and we’ll issue a credit or replacement.

Do you deliver outside Nairobi?

Yes, we deliver across Kenya, Uganda, and Tanzania. Delivery fees vary by location and order size.

Can I buy just one bale to test?

Absolutely. We encourage first‑time buyers to start with a single bale. If you’re happy with the quality, we offer discounts on subsequent orders.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept M‑Pesa, bank transfer, and major credit cards. For bulk orders, we offer 50% upfront and 50% on delivery.

How quickly can I get my order?

Nairobi deliveries: 2–3 days. Other regions: 3–5 days. Priority delivery available for retailers.

Do you offer credit terms for repeat buyers?

Yes, after 3 successful orders, you may qualify for our flexible payment program. Contact us to discuss options.
