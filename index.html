Frequently Asked Questions

What does "Grade A" mean?

Grade A bales contain high‑quality, near‑new items with minimal wear, no stains, and no defects. They are the best choice for resellers who want premium stock.

How long does delivery take?

Within Nairobi: 1–2 business days. Other regions: 2–4 business days. You'll receive a tracking number once your order ships.

Can I see the bales before buying?

Yes! We provide real photos and videos of each bale on WhatsApp. You can also visit our Nairobi warehouse by appointment.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept M‑Pesa, bank transfer, and major credit/debit cards. For bulk orders, we offer flexible payment plans.

Is there a minimum order?

No minimum order for first‑time buyers. For repeat buyers, we encourage larger orders for better rates, but you can still order single bales.

How do I track my order?

Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking number (e.g., KB-2026-0001). Use our Track Order page or message us on WhatsApp for the latest status.
